The theme system

Ten themes, one token contract

Every theme is a complete, type-checked Theme object. Reading-page contrast meets WCAG AA, highlights stay legible in every mode, and selection and read-along fills are semi-transparent so text shows through. Copy any one theme, or the whole file.

Export · all themes

Everything stays on your device
